## 📐 Best Settings for Image Generators

* **Aspect Ratio:** `1:1` (Square, perfect for 512x512 Play Store icons and app launchers).
* **Reference Images:** Use the original AURA logo (`Aura_logo_bg_removed.png`) and the Nimbo logo (`AURA_logo_new_black.png`) as image-to-image prompts at ~35%–45% image strength.
* **Negative Prompt / Constraints:** `text, typography, letters except A, watermark, blurry, realistic photo frame, multiple icons, complex background`.
